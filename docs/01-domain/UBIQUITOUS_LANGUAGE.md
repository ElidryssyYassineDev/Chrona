# Ubiquitous Language

## Purpose

This document defines the official business vocabulary used throughout Chrona.

Every term has one meaning and one name.

The same terminology must be used consistently in:

- Documentation
- Domain Model
- APIs
- Database
- Frontend
- Source Code
- Tests

---

# Core Concepts

## Tenant

An organization using Chrona.

Examples:
- ABC Consulting
- Acme Digital
- Tech Solutions Ltd.

A tenant owns all business data.

---

## Employee

A person employed by a tenant who performs work.

Employees belong to one tenant.

Employees may belong to one or more teams.

---

## Team

A group of employees working together.

Examples:

- Backend Team
- HR Team
- Design Team

---

## Department

A business unit within a tenant.

Examples:

- Engineering
- Human Resources
- Finance

---

## Project

A business initiative requiring work.

Projects contain work allocations.

Projects have planned capacity.

---

## Work Allocation ⭐

The planning decision that reserves an employee's capacity for a project during a specific period.

An allocation answers:

WHO

works

ON WHAT

FOR HOW LONG

WHEN

Example:

Alice is allocated 20 hours to Project Apollo next week.

---

## Assignment

A concrete piece of work assigned to an employee.

Assignments execute a work allocation.

Examples:

- Build Authentication Module
- Design Dashboard
- Write API Tests

---

## Capacity

The amount of work an employee can perform during a period.

Capacity is planned.

Time is actual.

---

## Availability

The amount of capacity remaining after accounting for:

- Existing allocations
- Leave
- Holidays

---

## Time Entry

A single record of work performed.

Example:

Tuesday

09:00–11:30

Authentication Module

2.5 hours

---

## Timesheet

A collection of time entries for a reporting period.

Usually weekly.

Submitted for approval.

---

## Approval

A business decision validating a request.

Approval does not modify history.

It changes business status.

---

## Leave Request

A request for planned absence.

Examples:

Vacation

Sick Leave

Training

Remote Work (optional policy)

---

## Audit Log

An immutable record of significant business actions.

Audit logs are never edited.

---

## Notification

A message generated by the system to inform users about business events.

Notifications do not contain business logic.

---

# Reserved Terms

These words have fixed meanings.

Allocation ≠ Assignment

Capacity ≠ Availability

Time Entry ≠ Timesheet

Department ≠ Team

Approval ≠ Authorization

Employee ≠ User

Tenant ≠ Company

---

# Naming Rules

Business terminology always takes precedence over technical terminology.

Example:

Use

WorkAllocation

Instead of

ProjectAssignmentEntity

---

# Living Language

This glossary evolves with the domain.

Any new business concept must be added before implementation.
